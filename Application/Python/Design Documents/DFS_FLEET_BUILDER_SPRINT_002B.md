# DFS Fleet Builder — Sprint 002B

## Purpose

This sprint connects the Fleet Construction Foundation to the first usable desktop workspace. The GUI remains a thin client over `FleetConstructionService`, `PlatformCatalogService`, `PlatformDetailService`, and `JSONFleetStore`.

## Included

- Fleet identity and construction controls
- Faction, fleet/era, year, priority, and FAP selection
- Legal platform browsing
- Fleet roster editing
- Live summary and validation
- Portable JSON save/load

## Deferred to Sprint 002C

- Purchase groups and multi-ship purchase units
- Fighter replacements and carried-craft choices
- Fleet-specific restrictions and allied allowances
- Ancient and Special priority equivalencies
- Roster PDF/print output
- Points values for game systems that provide them
